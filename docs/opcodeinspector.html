FFXIV .dat packet sniffer

Drop a recording to read every server-zone IPC packet it contains — opcode, size, timing and a raw hex view of the bytes. Built to hunt down the two opcodes the workbench has to hand-enter each patch: PartyList and PartyPortraitInfo. Everything runs in your browser; the file never leaves your machine.

Custom Opcode Finder

—

The finder reads the party's 8‑byte contentIds out of the (named) PlayerSpawn packets, then looks for the opcodes that carry several of them in a single payload — which is what a roster packet does and what a per‑actor packet like PlayerSpawn never does. Party names are a secondary signal, also counted per packet. PartyPortraitInfo is the 1408‑byte (8×176) appearance packet, and carries no name text at all; PartyList is the large (~3672‑byte) HUD roster packet. Confirm a guess by clicking it — the hex view highlights the names in the bytes.
